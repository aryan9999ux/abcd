FAQ

Straight answers

Is the account real money?

No. Accounts use simulated capital on our own terminal. The fee you pay is a platform fee. Rewards are paid by Apex Capital Funded under the Terms.

Is Apex Capital Funded a broker?

No. We do not execute orders on any exchange and we do not hold client securities. Check the Terms for the exact scope of the service.

Where do the prices come from?

The terminal shows market prices from a data provider. The preview on this page uses demo prices.

What happens if I breach a limit?

The account closes at the breach and you can start a new one. Limits are shown live on your dashboard and terminal.

Can I run more than one account?

Yes. Buy as many accounts as you like from your dashboard and switch between them in the terminal.
